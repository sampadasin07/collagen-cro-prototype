About this prototype

This is a responsive HTML prototype of an improved product page for Wellbeing Nutrition’s Pure Korean Marine Collagen Peptides. It implements the phase-1 changes from my CRO strategy and demonstrates the main purchase journey: product page → pack choice → checkout → confirmation. Use Show what changed on the product page or checkout to see each change and the friction it addresses.

The three priority frictions

#FrictionWhat the buyer meets on the current page
1The evidence isn’t believable“Backed by 20+ Clinical Studies” sits above “claims are ingredient-based and not for the product”; results promised in 4–8 weeks, 8–12 weeks, 12 weeks and 3–4 months; absorption claimed as 4x, 1.5x and 5x; two nutrition panels disagree; 4.74★ from 571 ratings with no one- or two-star reviews.
2The real commitment is hiddenOne tub lasts about 25 days but the label asks for a minimum of 3–4 months; packs are priced linearly and every one says “Save 20%”; no cost per day.
3Checkout adds pressure and removes controlOne pre-ticked box bundling order updates with offers, a red warning when unticked, a “might run Out of Stock” exit pop-up, and “Your cart is empty” after Buy It Now.

The five interventions, ranked by ICE

ICE = (Impact + Confidence + Ease) ÷ 3. Scores are judgement on a written rubric, not measured effects.

#InterventionICEIn this prototype
1Consistent evidence module7.0One label usage line; quick facts; ingredient studies separated from product lab reports; one nutrition panel (values [V]); multipliers removed
2Regimen-based pack selector7.0Packs labelled by duration, “about ₹58 a day”, 4 tubs “Recommended”, 1 tub default, prices unchanged
3Transparent price and offer block6.7Headline ₹1,439, “₹1,367 when you pay online”, one “Offers that apply” block, free shipping shown
4Suitability and safety panel6.7Non-veg and fish, allergy, doctor note, best-before [V], replacement policy, vegan link under the price
5Believable reviews6.3Rating summary and a real review near the buy box, dates shown, “Critical” filter

Intervention 1 goes first: it fixes the credibility problem at the core of the decision, uses wording the brand has already approved, and the pack selector depends on it. Factual and compliance fixes (stock count, exit pop-up, “allergen-free”, publisher names, consent box, cart error) ship regardless of ranking.

What the [V] placeholders mean

[V] marks a fact that hasn’t been confirmed yet. It is shown as a placeholder rather than a guessed value:

  • Nutrition values per serving (the live page shows two panels that disagree, e.g. protein 7.9 g vs 7.45 g).
  • Online prices for 2–4 tubs are shown using the same 5% prepaid discount framing as the 1-tub price; final payment rules should be confirmed with the brand.
  • Offer-stacking rules and the terms of the “Get 10% OFF” offer.
  • The batch best-before date.
  • Review dates, and the text of the single 3★ rating.
  • Whether 100 days “covers the 3–4 month course” (it covers the 3-month minimum).
  • Lab-report links, full study citations and the publisher of Orthopedic Reviews.

Deliberately not built

  • Course discount, subscription, starter or sachet pack, and a 4-tub default (later tests, not phase 1).
  • Pincode delivery date.
  • Amazon or Nykaa reviews.
  • Autoplay video, sign-up pop-up and spin-to-win pop-up.
  • Backend, real payments, real A/B testing or analytics.

The FAQ shows a short subset of the live page’s questions; answers that gave a different results timeline were aligned to the pack-label usage line. Product photos are replaced with an illustration.

No results are claimed

This prototype shows how the changes would work. It does not show that conversion would improve. Test 1 (evidence module) would measure add-to-cart rate, and Test 2 (pack selector, framing only) would measure revenue per visitor, each with guardrails on purchase and COD conversion, on the real site with real traffic.

Prices are as observed on the live page on 21 Oct 2026.

Replacement policy (as observed on the live site)

Replacements are offered only if the item is damaged in transit, defective or incorrect, reported with photos within 48 hours of delivery. Opened or used products can’t be returned.
