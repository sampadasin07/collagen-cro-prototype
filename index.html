Frequently asked questions

What are marine collagen peptides?

Short chains of amino acids made from fish skin or scales, rich in Type I and Type III collagen.

How much should I take, and for how long?

One 8 g scoop daily, minimum 3–4 months.

Can I mix it into hot drinks?

Yes. It dissolves in hot or cold drinks such as coffee, tea, juice or smoothies.

Can vegetarians use this collagen?

No. This product is made from fish, so it is not suitable for vegetarians. See our vegan collagen.
